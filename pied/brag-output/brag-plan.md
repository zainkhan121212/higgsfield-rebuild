# Pied — brag plan

**What it is:** Pied turns a photograph or a sentence into a picture made of thousands of letters that scatter when your cursor passes, then keeps it as a live desktop wallpaper.
**For:** anyone who wants a wallpaper that isn't a still image — and a reviewer seeing it for the first time.
**Sets it apart:** the picture is live type (not a filter), it can be lit as an LED board, and the wallpaper keeps reacting on the desktop.
**Hook:** an old painting dissolves into letters in the first second.
**Tone:** polished — restrained, print-shop black and white, colour only from the pictures.
**Format:** vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, 21 s.
**Identity (from the code):** paper #f4f3ee, ink #0c0c0b; Libre Caslon Display / Text, Courier Prime labels; figure numbers and roman numerals.
**Share caption:** see share-copy.txt.

## Sound
Composed for the video: a slow piano-and-pad progression (A minor → F → C → G), soft typewriter clicks under the dissolve, a muted key-clack at every style change, a low swell into the outro and a ringing final chord. Effects are pitched and placed inside the music, mixed under it.

## Checks
No invented features, numbers or testimonials: every feature shown exists in the code; the Lahore weather in scene 4 is the site's own demo sky.
