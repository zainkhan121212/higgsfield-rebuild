# Walkthrough script (≤ 5 min, camera on)

Open a **fresh incognito window** at https://higgsfield-rebuild-delta.vercel.app so the judges see the same first-run experience they'll get.

## 0:00 — What this is (20s)
"This is Higgsfield.ai rebuilt in a day. Same product shape — two studios, presets, an asset library, credits, pricing — with one deliberate change: on the original, a free account can't press Generate without hitting a paywall. Here every visitor gets 100 credits and a working account the moment the page loads. No sign-up form."

## 0:20 — Explore (30s)
Scroll the home page: feature cards, tool tiles, the Effects gallery (every tile is a still generated by this app), the public feed at the bottom. Hover a feed item → prompt/model appear. Click one → the share page, "Make your own".

## 0:50 — Image studio (75s)
- Click a suggestion or type a prompt. Point at the Generate button: cost shown before you click, struck-through list price like the original.
- Open the model picker (`⌘K`): featured / all, search, badges. Pick Soul Cinema. Change ratio 16:9, batch 2.
- `⌘+Enter`. Shimmer → real FLUX images that match the prompt, credits drop live in the nav. Click an image → lightbox, download.
- Favorite it, copy the share link.

## 2:05 — Video studio (60s)
- Left panel mirrors the original: preset card, References/Extend, drop zone, prompt, model row, duration/ratio/resolution chips, bitrate.
- "Change" → preset picker: model tabs, search, 20 presets with generated sample frames. Pick Wild Ride.
- Generate. Say out loud: **video models are simulated in this build** — the job pipeline, credits, polling, refunds on failure are real; the clip is a sample. No free text-to-video API fit in 24h, and this is labelled everywhere.

## 3:05 — Assets (25s)
All / Favorites / by type, grid slider, create a folder, move a generation into it.

## 3:30 — Accounts, credits & checkout (45s)
Nav shows Login / Sign up while you're a guest. Sign up (email + password) → the guest's credits and generations carry over, avatar appears. Pricing page: monthly/annual, credit sliders, one-off packs, plan wizard. Choose Pro → checkout: order review → card form (test card 4242…) → processing → receipt with order number; balance updates live in the nav. Account page shows plan, balance, transactions.

## 4:15 — What was left out & how it was built (45s)
- Left out on purpose: Audio, Edit/Layers, Motion Control, Canvas, MCP, Marketing Studio, Academy, Community, Enterprise, real payments.
- Stack: Next.js 16, Prisma + Supabase Postgres, Vercel (Sydney, next to the DB), Pollinations FLUX for images.
- Repo: recon notes, plan with the hour-by-hour calls, `CAPTURE-TEST.md`, `.agent-logs/` committed with every step.

Stop at ~4:50.
